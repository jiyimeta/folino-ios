folino is a score viewer and player for performers.
It is designed to make score reading, playback, and part practice quick and accessible on Android phones and tablets.
Once you open a score, it stays easy to use offline, whether you are in rehearsal, on stage, or reviewing music on the go.

[Supported Formats]
Import MuseScore .mscz and .mscx files, MusicXML, and MIDI, then play them back while reading the score.

[Readable by Design]
Switch between vertical scrolling and page mode, then use zoom and cursor movement to quickly check the passage you need.
Show or hide individual parts so you can focus on the staff you need, even on a smaller phone screen.

[Practice Features]
Adjust volume per part, mute or solo individual staves, and change instruments for each part so you can focus on the line you want to hear.
Use A-B repeat, a metronome, and tempo control to work through difficult phrases with repetition.
Background playback is also supported, so you can keep listening while doing other things on your device.
Picture in Picture keeps the score visible in a mini-player while another app is in use, so you can follow along without switching back.

[Stay Organized]
Use playlists and tags to organize your scores by ensemble, event, rehearsal set, or any other workflow that fits your music life.
Group the pieces you need and open them quickly for rehearsal, performance, or review.

[Key Features]
- Score display: vertical scroll / page mode
- Zoom and playback cursor movement
- MIDI playback
- Per-part volume, mute, and solo
- Per-part instrument selection
- A-B repeat
- Metronome
- Tempo control
- Import .mscz, .mscx, MusicXML, and MIDI
